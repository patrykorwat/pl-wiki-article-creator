# International Organization Sources for SOGI/LGBTQ Topics

Non-European sources for claims about anti-LGBTQ discrimination, hate speech, and human rights violations.

## UN Human Rights Council

### Resolution 17/19 (2011)
- **Title**: Human rights, sexual orientation and gender identity
- **Symbol**: A/HRC/RES/17/19
- **URL**: https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/707199/files/A_HRC_RES_17_19-EN.pdf
- **Key quote**: "Expressing grave concern at acts of violence and discrimination, in all regions of the world, committed against individuals because of their sexual orientation and gender identity"
- **Use for**: UN recognition of SOGI-based violence and discrimination as a human rights concern

### Resolution 32/2 (2016)
- **Title**: Protection against violence and discrimination based on sexual orientation and gender identity
- **Symbol**: A/HRC/RES/32/2
- **URL**: https://digitallibrary.un.org/record/845552/files/A_HRC_RES_32_2-EN.pdf
- **Use for**: Establishment of the Independent Expert on SOGI mandate

## Organization of American States (OAS)

### AG/RES. 2807 (2013)
- **Title**: Human rights, sexual orientation, and gender identity
- **Use for**: Condemnation of discrimination based on SOGI in the Americas

## African Commission on Human and Peoples' Rights (ACHPR)

### Resolution 275 (2014)
- **Title**: Resolution on Protection against Violence and other Human Rights Violations against Persons based on their Real or Imputed Sexual Orientation or Gender Identity
- **Key content**: Condemns violence and human rights violations based on sexual orientation; calls on states to enact and enforce laws prohibiting such violence
- **Note**: ACHPR website (achpr.au.int) may return SSL errors. Try chr.up.ac.za or Internet Archive as fallback.

## Joint UN Statement (2015)
- 12 UN entities jointly condemned violence and discrimination based on SOGI
- **Use for**: Demonstrating UN system-wide concern

## Demographic Data

### Williams Institute, UCLA School of Law
- Gates (2011): ~3.5% of US adults identify as LGB
- 2020-2021 BRFSS data: ~5.5% of US adults identify as LGBT
- **URL**: https://williamsinstitute.law.ucla.edu/publications/how-many-people-lgbt/
- **Use for**: Population prevalence estimates for LGBT identification

## Academic Sources for Organization Classification

### Ordo Iuris
- Curanović (2021) "The International Activity of Ordo Iuris" - Religions journal. Describes OI as conservative pro-life legal organization and part of global Christian right.
- Bustikova (2025) "Kill the Bill: Ordo Iuris and the Ratification of the Istanbul Convention" - Journal of Illiberalism Studies. Describes OI as "Polish Catholic conservative legal network."
